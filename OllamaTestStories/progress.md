## 20260701_215502 claude_diversity_check
- With `think: false` fixed, checked narrative diversity. 4 clean runs at temp 0.55 all converged on "The Zenith [X]" titles and a "Kaelen is secretly a non-human AI/archive" reveal.

- Conclusion: JSON reliability and motif diversity are separate problems with separate cheap fixes (`think: false`, code-authored anti-motif block) — a second LLM pass may not be necessary. ## 20260701_224541 claude_current_prod_check (post-Codex-fix verification)
- Codex landed `think: false`, `keep_alive: 0` for large_story, deterministic creative lanes + anti-motif guidance, and a repair/validator pass (commit `2f1ffbac`).
- Re-verified against the exact current `NarrativeDirector.build_campaign_bible_prompt()` output across 6 seeds spanning 4 different creative lanes: 6/6 valid JSON, 6/6 compliant `kaelen_rule` (broker/fixer/contract handler), 6/6 distinct titles and reveals, zero repeats of "Zenith [X]" pattern or Kaelen-as-AI reveal, zero banned motifs. ~22-26s each with `keep_alive: 0`. Matches and extends the reported 3/3 result.
- Found two remaining gaps by reading the landed code, not yet hit live: (1) `NarrativeDirector._repair_kaelen_public_role()` has an early-return bug — when `kaelen_rule` explicitly states a *violating* public role (e.g. "Kaelen publicly appears as a station AI"), the function detects it but returns without repairing it, so validation then hard-rejects the whole bible instead of the repair salvaging it. Traced by hand with a concrete repro string; did not reproduce live in 6 samples since the prompt is explicit enough that gemma4 rarely violates it now. (2) No automatic retry-once on validation failure was added — a hard failure leaves the loading screen stuck showing the debug reason with no in-app retry path (only a full app restart re-triggers generation). Low practical risk given (1) is rare and structural reliability is now ~6/6, but worth closing for robustness.
