## 1. Tags must describe the card (Section 7: "Tags describe the card honestly")

Two cards in batch_29 carry theme tags that look chosen to fill the deck
report's gaps rather than to say what the story is about. That breaks the
report that steers you: it now thinks those gaps are filled. Retag from the
story, not from the WRITE TOWARD line.

## 2. premise.the_heirloom_yeast (batch_29)

A lovely card; only its tags are wrong. It has exactly the tone and themes of
the_runaway_slate (`noir/melancholy`, `cost_of_neutrality/labour_and_exploitation`),
which don't fit a story about saving an eighty-year-old starter from
foreclosure. Pick tags from what it is about (for example `legacy`,
`who_owns_the_past`, `debt_and_obligation`; a gentler tone than noir).

## 3. premise.the_counterfeit_spoilage (batch_29)

- **Themes:** `reinvention` doesn't describe a staged blight and stolen
  grain. Retag honestly (for example `price_of_profit`, `helpers_corrupted`).
- **Beat 4:** `pickup_special` is a hand-over from a person at a dock; "collect
  the encrypted ledger from the terminal" has nobody handing it over. Have
  someone give it to the pilot (a clerk, the agronomist bargaining), or use
  another verb.
- **The final choice** offers "Release the impounded grain to the miners",
  but the pilot holds the ledger, not the grain. Make that option something
  the pilot can do with what they hold, or show the grain coming into their
  hands earlier.
- Resolution `trust_shattered`: "arrested on the tarmac" doesn't fit a
  station; say where.
