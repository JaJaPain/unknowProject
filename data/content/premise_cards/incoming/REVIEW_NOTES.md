## 1. Resting engines (check every card against this)

Insurance fraud; apprentice/junior leads; counterfeit or adulterated drink;
rigged lotteries; secret routes around toll monopolies; a faked hazard or
condemnation to seize property (including withholding protection, and
manufactured defaults to clear tenants); sabotaged navigation beacons or
faked sensor data; scanner false-positives on harmless things; substitutes
swapped in to hide an accident or theft; cornering fuel, coolant or food to
strand or squeeze people; buying people's debt to force indentures; secretly
diverting water in a drought; an old man undoing his past harm; refusing
rescue to protect something; an inheritance with a condition; two good
causes competing for one scarce thing; a new rule or standard secretly
lobbied for by whoever profits from it; a neutral party squeezed by both
sides for the evidence it holds; a word or idiom that means different
things to two sides; officers faking a wartime loss for money; a holy relic
that is really dangerous hardware; something valuable hidden inside an
innocent object; the neutral party is secretly the guilty one; a reformed
criminal secretly back at it; a safety override or structural shortcut
about to fail; a criminal hiding behind religious vows; an old document
that proves a claim fraudulent; a stolen or buried patent; a crew or
colony that faked its own loss to disappear.

Every card must also differ in engine from every other card you write in
the same session, **and from the cards approved in the last two rounds**
(the deck report lists them; the triage similarity list is a good check).
Never echo a Destination's secret (the Silent Fleet, the Lighthouse, the
Humming Gate, the Cartographer's Mine, the Garden Keepers, the Quiet War,
the Hollow Market, the Last Shipyard, the Archive, the Neutral Ground, the
Halo, the Wellhead).

## 2. Regional cards: vary the shape

Most regional cards so far are "a courier hauls an object across three
systems while hunted". The deck has plenty. Regional cards should usually be
something else: a person moving between systems, a story that spreads
system to system (a rumour, an epidemic, a strike), a pursuit where the
player is the hunter, or a set of linked places the player has to visit in
any order. (the_corrosive_electrolyte_blight was a good example: it
spreads.)
