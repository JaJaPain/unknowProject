## 1. Five cards repeat an engine; rework or retire each

For each: take it somewhere genuinely different (keep the setting and people
if you like; change what the story is about and why it happens), or retire
it and write a new card in its place. Keep the ids if you rework.

- **premise.the_stolen_chime_array** (batch_40): the curator staged the theft
  to claim insurance. Insurance fraud is resting.
- **premise.the_procession_beacon** (batch_40): someone re-angles navigation
  buoys to wreck ships, the same device as `the_skewed_heading` (approved).
- **premise.the_evacuee_exodus** (batch_41): a faked hazard to clear people
  off property. This run already had five of those (the condemned cavern,
  the air tariff, the terriers, the orchard, the silkworms); three were kept.
- **premise.the_orchard_swarm** (batch_42): a synthetic-food cartel ruins
  farms to buy them up, the same engine as `the_salted_beds` (approved), and
  another faked hazard.
- **premise.the_pedigree_embryo_vault** (batch_42): fakes swapped in to hide
  an accident, the same engine as `the_abbey_mash` (approved).

## 2. More engines to rest

Add to the resting list: **a faked hazard, condemnation or emergency used to
seize property or force people out**; **sabotaged navigation beacons or
buoys**; **substitutes swapped in to hide an accident**. Before writing each
card, name its engine in one line ("someone fakes X to get Y") and check it
against the cards you've written this session and the deck report's secrets.
