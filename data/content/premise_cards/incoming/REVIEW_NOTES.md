## 1. Resting engines (check every card against this)

Insurance fraud; apprentice/junior leads; counterfeit or adulterated drink;
rigged lotteries; secret routes around toll monopolies; a faked hazard or
condemnation to seize property (including withholding protection); sabotaged
navigation beacons or faked sensor data; scanner false-positives on harmless
things; substitutes swapped in to hide an accident or theft; cornering fuel,
coolant or food to strand or squeeze people; buying people's debt to force
indentures; secretly diverting water in a drought; an old man undoing his
past harm; refusing rescue to protect something; an inheritance with a
condition; two good causes competing for one scarce thing; a new rule or
standard secretly lobbied for by whoever profits from it; a neutral party
squeezed by both sides for the evidence it holds; a word or idiom that means
different things to two sides; officers faking a wartime loss for money;
a holy relic that is really dangerous hardware; something valuable hidden
inside an innocent object; the neutral party is secretly the guilty one;
a reformed criminal secretly back at it; a safety override or structural
shortcut about to fail.

Every card must also differ in engine from every other card you write in
the same session, **and from the cards approved in the last two rounds**
(the deck report lists them; the triage similarity list is a good check).

## 2. Regional cards: vary the shape

Five of this session's six regional cards were "a courier hauls an object
across three systems while hunted". It works, but the deck now has plenty.
Regional cards should usually be something else: a person moving between
systems, a story that spreads system to system (a rumour, an epidemic, a
strike), a pursuit where the player is the hunter, or a set of linked
places the player has to visit in any order.
