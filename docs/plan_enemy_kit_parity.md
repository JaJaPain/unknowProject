## Why This Matters

Right now enemies can only threaten the player in one direction: damage. The player has reactive tools (Shield Reroute, Brace, Micro-warp) but nothing to react *to* — enemies never do anything defensive. That makes fights feel one-dimensional: queue damage, execute, repeat.

Adding **Brace** and **Shield Angle** to enemies forces the player to read the telegraph and make a real decision each turn:
- Fire into a braced enemy for half damage, or switch to drone / hold AP?
- Fire normally or flank/warp first to bypass their Shield Angle?

This also sets up the boss (Phase 7) properly — a boss with no defensive kit is just a damage sponge.

---

## What Enemies Get

Two new defensive actions. Deliberately not mirroring everything the player has — enemies still lack Drone and Micro-warp. That keeps the player feeling capable.

### Brace
- **Who:** Gunner (smart variants), Interceptor (desperate situations)
- **AP cost:** 2 (meaningful — Gunner can brace OR fire twice, not both)
- **Effect:** All incoming damage this turn ×0.60 (40% reduction)
- **Drone exception:** Drone attack bypasses brace entirely (precision targeting)
- **Flank exception:** Flanking player halves the reduction (only 20% reduction, not 40%)
- **Duration:** Current turn only — clears at start of next planning phase
- **Player decision:** Fire for reduced damage? Switch to drone? Reposition and wait?

### Shield Angle
- **Who:** Interceptor (their defensive equivalent instead of Brace)
- **AP cost:** 1 (cheap — but prevents flanking from being free)
- **Effect:** First incoming player hit this turn reduced 65% (mirrors player Shield Reroute)
- **Bypass:** Player Micro-warp before firing nullifies it (angle changed). Drone bypasses it.
- **Duration:** Consumed on first hit, or clears at turn end
- **Player decision:** Flank first? Use drone? Just fire (eat the mitigation on a weak shot)?

## Archetype Matrix

| Archetype | Brace | Shield Angle | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gunner | ✓ intel ≥ 0.6, hp ≥ 50%, early rounds | ✗ | Offense-focus — brace is their one concession to defense |
| Interceptor | ✗ | ✓ intel ≥ 0.55, MID/LONG range | They reposition to dodge — shield angle is their static defense |
| Logistics | ✗ | ✗ | Already has repair — enough defensive tools |
| Hauler | ✗ | ✗ | They surrender, not fight |

---

## Player Counter-Play Table

| Enemy does | Best counter | Alt |
|---|---|---|
| Brace | Switch to Drone (bypasses) | Fire anyway (×0.60) + set up flank for next turn |
| Shield Angle | Micro-warp first, then fire (angle bypassed) | Drone (bypasses) |
| Repair | Commit to kill shot NOW | Disable engines → fire |
| Disable Engines | Shield Reroute (defensive turn, eat AP loss) | Fire before it lands |

## What This Unlocks for Phase 7 (Boss)

The boss can combine both in one plan:
`"⛨ Brace → ⚡ Shield angle → Heavy hull shot"`

That's a turn where the boss is nearly invulnerable AND hits hard. Player needs to see it in the telegraph and respond with Drone (bypasses brace) and then Micro-warp before firing (bypasses shield angle). A rich tactical puzzle that only works because both systems exist.
