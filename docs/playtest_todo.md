# Playtest checklist (Abe)

Things to check by hand in the running game. Every item can be done on
demand: nothing depends on a random roll or waiting for the story model.
Tick the box, and write what you saw under anything that looks wrong.
Claude adds new items here as work lands (newest section at the bottom).
