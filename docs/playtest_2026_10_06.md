# Playtest 2026-10-06

Findings recorded during play; fixes after Abe ends the test.

## 1. Tractor beam still too quiet

**Abe:** the tractor beam is still too quiet (it was raised 10% after the
2026-10-05 playtest).

**Research:**
- The docking hum is a 3D sound on the ship
  (`scripts/effects/DockingTractorBeam.gd`: `HUM_PLAYER_DB` -3.2 dB,
  `unit_size` 15, `max_db` 2, `max_distance` 350, SFX bus).
- Last time's "10% louder" was -4.0 to -3.2 dB, about +0.8 dB. That's barely
  audible; a clearly louder sound needs +4 to +6 dB, and "twice as loud" is
  about +10 dB.
- The bigger cause is likely distance. There is no AudioListener3D, so the
  listener is the camera, and the camera sits well back from the ship (the
  chase camera, plus zoom). With `unit_size` 15 and inverse-distance falloff,
  at ~150 m the hum is about 20 dB down before `volume_db` is applied.
  `max_distance` 350 m also cuts it off completely if the camera is zoomed
  far out.

**Proposed fix:** make the player's own beam ignore camera distance (a 2D
player, or a much larger `unit_size` / `max_distance` for the player's hum)
and raise it by about +5 dB; leave traffic beams 3D so other ships still fade
with distance. Check by ear in the dock smoke test or the station tour.

## 2. A click when the beam takes hold and when it lets go

**Abe:** there should be a click when the tractor beam takes hold, and the
same sound when it lets loose.

**Research:**
- No click or clamp sound exists in `sound/` (closest: none; the beam only
  has `sound/Mining/TractorBeam.mp3` as its hum).
- The beam is one node, `scripts/effects/DockingTractorBeam.gd`, so both
  moments have a single home: `configure()` (takes hold) and when the node
  leaves the tree (lets go). It knows whether it's the player's ship
  (`is_player`), so traffic beams can stay silent or quieter.
- When the beam grabs and lets go during a visit (UIManager):
  - **Docking:** grabs in the dock procedure (~line 4583); lets go once the
    clamps are secure and the bay is pressurised (~line 4672). That release
    happens while the ship is berthed, as "CLAMPS SECURE" finishes.
  - **Undocking:** a new beam grabs at undock (~line 10874) and lets go at
    "Clear of the safety zone. Controls are yours." (main stations and
    outposts).
  - So a visit has four clicks: grab and release docking, grab and release
    undocking.

**Proposed fix:** one short mechanical click (a magnetic latch: a sharp
transient with a little low thump, under 0.3 s), played for the player's beam
on take-hold and on let-go, not affected by camera distance. No suitable
sound exists, so either generate it (a small synthesised WAV) or Abe sources
one. Question for Abe: should the docking beam's release (when the clamps
take over at the berth) click too, or only the undock release?

## 3. START NEW CAMPAIGN and RETIRE THE CAPTAIN are too easy to click

**Abe:** these two are extreme, game-ending decisions sitting casually among
the everyday buttons on the SYSTEMS menu. Keep them available, but gated
behind a different tab or button. Come up with an idea.

**Research:**
- Both sit in the COMMAND column (`UIManager.gd` ~2683-2689) between
  CAMPAIGNS & SAVES and QUIT TO DESKTOP, styled like every other action.
- START NEW CAMPAIGN doesn't delete anything: it opens a fresh slot, and the
  current campaign stays in its slot. It belongs with the campaign slots in
  CAMPAIGNS & SAVES, which already lists them.
- RETIRE THE CAPTAIN is the truly final one (keepsake, then the campaign and
  its saves are deleted). Today it's one click plus an OK dialog.

**Proposal (sent to Abe, awaiting his call):**
1. Take both off the COMMAND column.
2. START NEW CAMPAIGN moves into CAMPAIGNS & SAVES, as the action on an
   empty slot ("New campaign in this slot"), where choosing a slot already
   happens.
3. RETIRE moves to the bottom of CAMPAIGNS & SAVES, in a separate red-edged
   "End this campaign" area, away from the save buttons. It opens its own
   screen, not a dialog: what retiring means (the story is kept as a
   keepsake; the campaign and its saves are deleted), a glimpse of what's
   being closed (days flown, stories, credits), and a **hold-to-confirm**
   button ("Hold to retire the Captain", a bar filling over ~3 s; let go to
   cancel). Back is the default.

## 4. Fly to a station or outpost: orbit on arrival, never fly in too close

**Abe:** "Fly to" on a station or outpost should put you in orbit when you
arrive, unless you told it to dock. Autopilot should never fly too close.

**Research:**
- "Fly to" is nav mode `APPROACH` (`UIManager.gd` ~1177; `PlayerShip.gd`
  ~1274 steers straight at the target's centre).
- The stop distance (`PlayerShip.gd` ~1435): stations stop at a flat
  **110 m** from the centre; only a berthed main station uses its approach
  sphere. Outposts and other stations load scaled models much bigger than
  110 m (that's why routes used to fly through them; `ObstacleBounds.
  visual_radius` exists for exactly this), so arriving at 110 m from the
  centre is at or inside the hull.
- On arrival APPROACH just holds position (the speed controller settles at
  the stop distance); it never switches to an orbit.
- **ORBIT itself is broken for big targets:** its radius is a fixed
  **25 m** (`PlayerShip.gd` ~1368), so "Orbit" on a station or outpost
  circles inside the model.

**Proposed fix:**
- APPROACH to a station or outpost stops at the edge of what you can see:
  the visual radius plus a margin (the berthed main station keeps its
  approach sphere). On arrival it switches to ORBIT at that distance.
- ORBIT's radius comes from the target's size (visual radius + margin) for
  stations, outposts, gates and large objects; small things (asteroids,
  ships) keep a tight orbit.
- Docking (DOCK) is unchanged: it's the only autopilot order that goes in
  close.
- Check with a smoke test: fly to every station and outpost in the start
  systems, and assert the ship never comes inside the visual radius and ends
  up orbiting.

## 5. Haggling should be able to backfire

**Abe:** when you push an agent for more money, sometimes they should *lower*
the payout instead, with something cocky ("The pay's less now. Take the job
or don't. Don't waste my time."), a few variations. Pushing should be hit or
miss.

**Research:**
- A mission offer has three answers; the third is the push ("150 is
  garbage. Double the payout or mine it yourself."). Its `consequence` is
  fixed when the offer is made (`LLMInterface.gd` examples ~157-176, and
  `data/content/llm_dialogue_content.json`): `reward_credits_multiplier`
  1.5-1.6, `combat_multiplier` 1.7, reputation -5, and a line that always
  agrees ("Payout is bumped..."). So pushing **always** works today.
- The multiplier is read at payout (`QuestManager.active_quest_payout`,
  ~906), and `MissionConsequenceDefinition` already allows a multiplier
  down to 0.5, so a cut needs no new plumbing.
- The answer's consequence is applied on accept (`QuestManager.gd` ~627),
  so a roll can happen right there, in code, independent of whether the
  model or a template wrote the offer.

**Proposed fix:**
- When the player picks the push answer, roll: roughly 60% it works as now;
  roughly 40% the agent cuts the pay instead (multiplier ~0.8), the
  reputation hit still lands, the extra danger doesn't, and the agent's
  reply is replaced by a cocky refusal.
- Refusal lines are authored, never the model: a handful for faction agents
  and a separate set in Kaelen's voice (she calls the Captain "Shiny").
  These go to Abe in a short batch for approval before they ship.
- Maybe weigh the odds by standing with that faction (better standing,
  better odds), so it's not pure luck. Abe to decide.
- Check: a test that forces both rolls and asserts the payout goes up or
  down and the right line is used.

## 6. Mining lasers stay on after "Dock at Station"

**Abe (screenshot):** pressing Dock at Station while the mining lasers are
running leaves them firing (two beams stretched back to the asteroid across
the whole flight) until you change course.

**Research:**
- The beams are only switched off by `_hide_mining_beams()` in specific
  places: when the hold fills, when MINE finds it can't mine or is out of
  range, and in `cancel_autopilot()` (which a manual course change calls).
- Pressing Dock calls `begin_target_navigation("DOCK")`, which just sets
  `nav_mode`. The `nav_mode` setter (`PlayerShip.gd` ~113) clears dock and
  jump state but never the mining beams. In DOCK mode nothing else touches
  them, so they stay drawn from the ship to the old rock (the green and blue
  lines in the screenshot), with the mining sound.
- The same is probably true of switching from mining straight to Fly to,
  Orbit or a gate approach.

**Proposed fix:** in the `nav_mode` setter, whenever the mode leaves MINE (or
ATTACK, which uses the same beams), call `_hide_mining_beams()`. One line,
covers every order. Test: start mining, press Dock, assert the beams are
hidden and the mining sound stopped on the next frame.
