### 1. Kaelen's lounge chat talks about the finished tutorial (step 4)

**Abe (screenshot, Greywake lounge):** Kaelen: "Shiny, you're still broke.
That Reaver's coming for you." Funny, but the Reaver was already killed and
the tutorial job handed in; this was after a second job (ore).

**Research:**
- It's a live lounge conversation with Kaelen's card (`UIManager`, the
  lounge chat around ~7100: `LLMInterface.request_lounge_chatter` with
  `_lounge_card_context(card)`), generated by the small model when you open
  her card (live log line 668).
- So the context the model gets still says the Captain is broke and that a
  Reaver is the threat: the tutorial's opening facts (the campaign bible's
  opening situation, or Kaelen's packet) aren't retired once the tutorial
  job is done.

**Proposed fix:** check `_lounge_card_context` / Kaelen's interaction packet
for tutorial-era facts (the Reaver, "broke", the starter debt) and drop them
once `first_contract_handed_in` is set; give her what's true now (credits,
jobs done, the current job). A test: after the tutorial, her lounge context
mentions no Reaver and no "broke" unless the Captain's credits are low.

### 2. N.O.V.A.'s double meanings come off weird, not flirty (all through)

**Abe:** she should sound flirty, but she's working too hard at the double
entendre, and it reads weirdo rather than flirty. She does it after attacks
as well.

**Lines from this session and the last (quiet-moment log, live log):**
- "Back to the same station. You'll know I'm here. My access ports feel
  like they've been waiting."
- "Sella Rusk's slow flush left me thinking I'd leak coolant. My lower
  ports felt cold for a while."
- "My plating's oiling, Captain. Let's see how long it takes you to hold
  the rag." (rejected for another reason)
- After fights: "My ribs are clean. The paint's chipped where they hit. Who
  else saw it? My frame spars, technically." (the deliberate body slip
  stacked on a suggestive line)

**Research:**
- `data/content/quiet_moment_beats.json`: almost every N.O.V.A. beat,
  including `nova_post_combat_damaged`, shares one "register" block: "The
  ship IS her body... entirely willing to make it sound more suggestive
  than it strictly needs to be", with maintenance vocabulary (access ports,
  flushing, hands on her). The small model leans on the body/port words
  every time, so it's suggestive in every line, in every situation.
- The example in the sense check ("Mrs. Kross had her hands on me for
  hours. Warm solvent, slow work.") is marked as fine and teaches the same.
- `NovaAnatomySlip` (the secret's body slips) adds "ribs", "knees" etc. on
  top, so after fights she gets both at once.

**Proposed fix (needs Abe's sign-off on the new tone, with sample lines):**
- Rewrite the register: flirty = confident, teasing, warm, aimed at the
  Captain's flying and attention ("You fly better when I'm watching"), not
  her parts being handled. Maintenance double meaning only in the
  maintenance/repair beats, and light (one soft word, not "ports",
  "flush", "lower", "leak").
- Post-combat, transit and back-at-the-station beats: no innuendo; dry wit
  and a bit of warmth.
- Never both in one line: no body slip on a line that's already teasing.
- Drop the "warm solvent" example from the sense check; teach the new tone.

### 3. Jenna makes things up (and overreaches like N.O.V.A.)

**Abe (screenshot, Maintenance Bay):** "Miner's engine's been sputtering
since last week, saw you crash-land near the ore fields. Money's tight, but
I'll get you back on track before the next hail." Same thing as N.O.V.A.'s
lines; and look for things she makes up that aren't known in game.

**Research:**
- `UIManager._build_mechanic_intro_prompt` (~10123). With no pickup in play
  her fact packet is only the ship's name, credits and a private faction
  note, but rule 3 says: "Make one concrete observation about the ship,
  the pilot's recent trouble, or money." There are no facts about recent
  trouble, so the model invents them ("crash-land near the ore fields",
  "sputtering since last week").
- Rule 4 asks for "a little too personal", the same push that makes
  N.O.V.A. overreach.

**Proposed fix:**
- Give her real facts to observe, from the game: hull % (and whether the
  last fight dented it), what was just fitted or is being saved for (the
  goal card), credits, the last job's kind and how it ended. Rule 3
  becomes "comment on ONE of these facts"; plus "never mention an event,
  place or time that isn't in the fact packet".
- A check on her line (like the sense check): reject lines that mention a
  crash, a past week, a place or a person not in the packet; retry, then
  fall back to her written lines.
- Tone: cocky and dry, not "a little too personal"; same new direction as
  finding 2.
- Look for the same "invent recent trouble" pattern in other prompts
  (agents' intros, lounge contacts, Kaelen's lounge chat in finding 1).

**Abe's tone sample for N.O.V.A. (finding 2), approved 2026-10-10:**
1. Back at a station: "Same station again. I'm starting to think you are
   getting addicted to the coffee."
2. After a clean fight: "Not a scratch. You're showing off now, aren't you?"
3. After a messy fight: "Dented, but I'm still pretty. Don't make a habit of
   it."
4. Long transit: "Long haul. Talk to me, Captain. I get bored when you go
   quiet."
5. After repairs: "All buffed out. Whoever did it took their time. I
   approve."
6. Back at a station: "You fly better when I'm watching. Lucky for you, I'm
   always watching."
These set her new register: flirty = confident, teasing, aimed at the
Captain; light, never about her parts being handled.
