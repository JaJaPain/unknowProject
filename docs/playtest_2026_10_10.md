### 5. Combat takes much longer to bring up the combat wheel (new)

**Abe:** combat takes a lot longer from engagement to the UI; so long that
both ships fly out of the camera before the combat wheel loads. New.

**Research (live log ~513-530, 733-749, 840-873):**
- `CombatManager.start_combat` requests the enemy's taunts from the small
  model and only `_on_taunts_ready` calls `_begin_planning()` (the wheel).
- Every fight this session: "combat taunts HTTP failed (result=13)" (a
  timeout) -> "retrying in 3s" -> fell back to canned lines. The taunt
  request's timeout is 8 s (`LocalModelGateway.REQUEST_TIMEOUTS
  ["kaelen_line"]`), so the wheel waits up to ~8 + 3 + 8 = 19 s.
- Why now: the small model is busier than before (lounge bundles, line
  banks, ambient chat, the new quiet-moment sense check), and Ollama answers
  one request at a time, so the taunt request queues and times out.
- Not ChatGPT's uncommitted CombatManager change: that's a whitespace-only
  edit to a comment.

**Proposed fix:** never hold the fight for taunts. Start planning at once
with the cached taunt pool for this faction/cause (`CombatManager` already
keeps 786 persisted lines), and swap in the live ones if they arrive; give
the combat taunt request priority in the gateway (ahead of background work)
and no retry once the fight has started. Check: the wheel appears within
about a second of engagement, with or without the model.

**Abe's call (finding 5):** one taunt per fight. After the opening taunt, no
more spoken (TTS) enemy lines, unless the player or the enemy flees (then
one line for that). So: the opening line can come from the cached pool at
once; the per-turn action taunts and other mid-fight voiced lines go.
Check what that leaves the live model request doing (probably nothing:
drop it, or keep it only to top up the pool after the fight).
