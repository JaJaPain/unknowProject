# New Destinations, draft batch 3 (for Abe's review)

The last pair from `docs/destinations_expansion_plan_2026_10_06.md`. Same parts
as every Destination card; nothing goes into the game until Abe approves the
lines. Both need new set pieces (briefs #11 and #12 in
`docs/set_piece_wishlist.md`), stand-ins until then.

One change from the plan: the Halo's covenant is "no one may be taken away
against their will" rather than "no weapons", so it doesn't echo the Neutral
Ground.

---

## 5. The Halo

*Director note (never shown):* a loose circle of small candle-lit pilgrim
chapels on tethers round a central bell-buoy, in a system whose red star is
dying. By an old covenant every faction still honours, anyone who rings the
bell is under sanctuary: no one may be taken from the Halo against their will.
Its keepers are a quiet order who tend the candles and keep the name of
everyone who has ever rung.

**The rumour:** "A bounty hunter came back empty-handed and furious: her mark
had 'rung the bell', and couldn't be touched."

**N.O.V.A., first time she hears it:** "A bell that makes you untouchable.
Either it's a very good story or a very strange law."

**Bearings** (clue / N.O.V.A.):
1. "Debt collectors keep a private list of names marked 'rang the bell', and
   never chase them again."
   / "A list of people even collectors give up on. I want to know what's on
   the other end of that."
2. "Pilgrims buy candles by the crate at the deep stations, and won't say what
   they're for."
   / "Crates of candles, going somewhere dark. Romantic, or a fire hazard."
3. "A fugitive turned herself in, then walked free when the court learned
   she'd spent a year at the Halo."
   / "Even the courts respect it. That's a lot of power for a bell."
4. "Navigators past the Class III gates mark one dying red star 'do not hunt
   here' in the margins of their charts."
   / "Do not hunt here. Hand-written. I like these navigators."
5. "The margin notes all point to one red star past the edge of the charts.
   The Halo circles it."
   / "There it is. Let's go and hear that bell."

**Arrival** (speaker: THE HALO):
- *N.O.V.A., on approach:* "Candles. Hundreds of little lights on tethers,
  round that red star. It's a ring of chapels, Captain."
- "Peace, traveller. You've come to the Halo. Whoever rings the bell here
  can't be taken away against their will. That's the whole of our law."
- "Debtors, runaways, the sorry and the hunted have all rung it. We don't ask
  what they did. We only keep their names, so they're never forgotten."
- "You haven't come to ring it, so you've come to see. Take our pilgrims'
  charts; they know the quiet roads."
- *N.O.V.A.:* "A whole sanctuary built on one promise, and everyone still
  keeps it. I didn't think the frontier had anything this gentle."
- *Farewell:* "The keepers say the first bell came from somewhere beyond the
  charts. Nobody here remembers where. Maybe you'll find it."

**Bridges** (why the season's culprit wants this place):
- *protect a secret child:* "Someone they love rang the Halo's bell and lives
  there under another name. As long as the covenant holds, nobody can take
  them."
- *escape a debt:* "Ring the bell and no collector can touch you. A debt that
  would follow them to the grave stops at the Halo's tethers."
- *rewrite a verdict:* "The Halo's keepers record every confession made under
  sanctuary. One of those confessions clears their family's name."
- *keep the lights on:* "A failing colony could ring the bell as one and
  claim sanctuary from its creditors. Someone needs the covenant to hold,
  whatever it costs."

## 6. The Wellhead

*Director note (never shown):* a small ice moonlet whose vent geyser waters the
whole frontier. One family runs the rig towers over the vent, and every water
contract in the region is signed at its table. Not villains: they've kept the
price fair for a century. They're tired, and the vent is slowing, and nobody
outside knows.

**The rumour:** "Every water contract in the frontier is stamped with the same
small seal: a drop over a crack."

**N.O.V.A., first time she hears it:** "Everyone's water, signed in one place.
Whoever holds that seal could make the whole frontier thirsty."

**Bearings** (clue / N.O.V.A.):
1. "Water haulers out past the gates all fill at the same depot, and all carry
   the same seal on their manifests."
   / "One seal on every water manifest. That's not a market, that's a tap."
2. "Old miners say the price of water hasn't changed in a hundred years, no
   matter how dry the year."
   / "A fair price for a century. Somebody out there is either a saint or very
   careful."
3. "Ice dust drifts down the deep lanes in a slow stream that always comes
   from the same direction."
   / "Ice drifting downstream from one place. Follow the water."
4. "A hauler swears the depot's tanks were half empty, for the first time in
   living memory."
   / "Half empty. For the first time. And nobody's said a word about it."
5. "The ice stream leads to one moonlet past the edge of the charts. The
   Wellhead is there."
   / "There it is. The tap the whole frontier drinks from."

**Arrival** (speaker: THE WELLHEAD):
- *N.O.V.A., on approach:* "A little ice moon with a plume coming off it, and
  towers all over it, Captain. That's the Wellhead."
- "Welcome to the Wellhead. Every drink of water on the frontier starts here,
  under our feet."
- "My family has signed every water contract for a hundred years and never
  raised the price. Not once. People forget that."
- "Here's something nobody else knows: the vent is slowing. Take our survey of
  the deep ice fields; someone will have to find the next well."
- *N.O.V.A.:* "The whole frontier's water rests on one tired family. I didn't
  expect to feel sorry for them."
- *Farewell:* "If the next well is out there, it's past the charts. Find it
  before the frontier finds out we're running dry."

**Bridges:**
- *own the lanes:* "Whoever controls the Wellhead's seal controls every water
  hauler in the region, and every lane they fly."
- *buy a station:* "A station that can't get water is worth nothing. Stop its
  contracts at the Wellhead, then buy it for the price of a drink."
- *keep the lights on:* "A failing colony needs the Wellhead's water more than
  anyone, and the vent is slowing. Someone will take it, whatever it costs
  everyone else."
- *start a war:* "Tell two factions the other is buying up the last of the
  Wellhead's water, and they'll fight over the tap. Someone profits from
  both."
