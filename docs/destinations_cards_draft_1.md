# New Destinations, draft batch 1 (for Abe's review)

Two of the six from `docs/destinations_expansion_plan_2026_10_06.md`, the two
that reuse existing set pieces. Each has the same parts as the six already in
the game (see `data/content/lodestars.json`). Nothing goes into the game
until Abe approves the lines.

**Status 2026-10-06: approved by Abe and in the game** (`data/content/lodestars.json`,
`lodestar_bridges.json`), with one change: the Market's first arrival line is
now "You've found the Market. No flags, no names, no questions." (Abe: the
original was too wordy).

---

## 1. The Hollow Market

*Director note (never shown):* an ungoverned bazaar bored into the core of a
long-period comet that swings through the deep systems. No flag, no law, no
records; debts and names can be bought clean there. Its keepers re-bore the
openings every pass so no fleet can take it.

**The rumour:** "A trader paid her docking fee in coin no station mints, and
said she got it inside a comet."

**N.O.V.A., first time she hears it:** "Inside a comet. Either she's lying,
or someone out there is running a shop in a snowball. I'd like to know which."

**Bearings** (clue / N.O.V.A.):
1. "Goods with no registry stamp keep surfacing in the deep markets, all
   packed in the same ice-cut crates."
   / "Same crates, no stamps. One supplier, and they don't want to be found."
2. "A debtor everyone thought ruined walked into a station with a clean name
   and new papers. He'd been 'out at the Market'."
   / "A new name, bought clean. That's either wonderful or terrifying."
3. "Pilots past the Class III gates report a comet that answers hails with a
   price list."
   / "A comet with a price list. I've officially heard everything."
4. "The comet's orbit is on old charts, but its openings are never in the
   same place twice: the keepers re-bore them every pass."
   / "They move the doors so nobody can lay siege. Clever. Paranoid, but
   clever."
5. "The comet's next pass brings it through one system past the edge of the
   charts. The Hollow Market is there."
   / "Found it. Keep a hand on your wallet, Captain."

**Arrival** (speaker: THE HOLLOW MARKET):
- *N.O.V.A., on approach:* "A comet with lights inside. Those are lanterns,
  Captain. People live in there."
- "Ship off the chart, you've found the Market. No flags past the ice line,
  no names you didn't bring, and nobody asks where your money's been."
- "Everything's for sale here: fuel, parts, a clean record, new papers. Just
  remember everything has a price, and we always collect."
- "You came the long way, so here's something free: a route the haulers use
  when they don't want to be followed. Don't say where you got it."
- *N.O.V.A.:* "A whole economy nobody owns. I hate it. I want to see every
  shop."
- *Farewell:* "Before you go: the comet's next pass runs further out than any
  chart. Some of our best customers come from there."

**Bridges** (why the season's culprit wants this place; one is drawn):
- *escape a debt:* "At the Market a name can be bought clean and a debt sold
  to people who never collect. One trip there, and the debt that would follow
  them to the grave dies instead."
- *own the lanes:* "Half the region's untaxed cargo moves on the Market's
  hidden routes. Whoever controls its keepers controls the lanes nobody
  watches."
- *buy a station:* "A station drowning in debt can have its paper bought at
  the Market for almost nothing, by whoever made sure it was drowning."
- *break a rival:* "At the Market anything is for sale, including a rival's
  secrets, debts and invented crimes. Buy enough of them and the rival is
  nobody."

## 2. The Last Shipyard

*Director note (never shown):* a drydock where one family of shipwrights has
spent four generations building a single enormous ship, the Long Promise, to
their great-grandmother's plans. It has never flown. The family has argued
for decades about whether it should, and who gets to say so.

**The rumour:** "A hauler swears she saw a ship being built in deep space so
big it had docking lights before it had engines."

**N.O.V.A., first time she hears it:** "A ship bigger than a station, still
unfinished, out past the gates. Someone's been building something for a very
long time."

**Bearings** (clue / N.O.V.A.):
1. "Deep foundries keep filling orders for hull plate in one odd gauge nobody
   has built ships with in a century."
   / "Somebody's building to an old pattern. A very old, very big pattern."
2. "An old engineer in a dockside bar carries her grandmother's plans for a
   ship 'longer than a gate is wide'."
   / "Longer than a gate is wide. So it can never leave. Unless it was never
   meant to."
3. "Haulers who deliver to the far yards are paid in carved fittings and
   promises, never in credits."
   / "Paid in promises. Whoever runs that yard has more faith than money."
4. "Welding light shows on the far lanes every shift, every year: someone has
   never stopped working."
   / "Four generations of welders. That's not a job, that's a family vow."
5. "The plate orders all ship to one system past the edge of the charts. The
   Last Shipyard is there."
   / "There it is. Let's go and see a ship nobody's finished."

**Arrival** (speaker: THE LAST SHIPYARD):
- *N.O.V.A., on approach:* "Look at the size of that hull, Captain. And it's
  still in scaffolding."
- "Mind the sparks, stranger. You're looking at the Long Promise. My
  great-grandmother laid her keel, and we've been building her ever since."
- "Half the family says she's ready to fly. The other half says she was
  never meant to. We vote every year, and nobody wins."
- "You've seen the lanes out there. Tell us honestly: is there anywhere left
  worth flying her to? Take our charts in trade for your answer."
- *N.O.V.A.:* "Four generations, one ship, and they still can't agree what
  it's for. I love these people."
- *Farewell:* "If you find somewhere worth sailing to, come back and tell us.
  We'll finish her faster."

**Bridges:**
- *take the chair:* "The yard's family elects a head every year, and the seat
  was promised to someone else. Whoever holds it decides whether the Long
  Promise ever flies."
- *prove them wrong:* "Every guild and registry said the great ship could
  never fly. Launch it, and every one of them is wrong in front of the whole
  frontier."
- *keep the lights on:* "A ship that size could carry a failing colony away,
  or keep one alive on its reactors. Someone needs it, whatever it costs the
  family."
- *protect a secret child:* "Someone they love is hidden in the shipwright
  family under another name. Keep the yard closed, and they stay hidden."
