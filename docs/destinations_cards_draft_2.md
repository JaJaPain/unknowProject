## 3. The Archive

*Director note (never shown):* an order of record-keepers on a station
drifting in a gas giant's upper clouds. For generations they have copied every
registry, deed and court ruling filed in the region, including the ones that
were destroyed. They take no sides and sell nothing: a copy costs only an
honest answer to why you need it.

**The rumour:** "A court on the frontier threw out a deed as forged, and the
owner produced a certified copy no court had ever seen. 'From the Archive,' he
said."

**N.O.V.A., first time she hears it:** "A copy nobody knew existed, of a paper
everybody swore was fake. Somebody out there keeps everything."

**Bearings** (clue / N.O.V.A.):
1. "Court clerks in three systems describe the same visitor: a quiet copyist
   who asks for nothing but a look at the ledgers."
   / "Someone's reading every court ledger in the region. Politely."
2. "A family won back land they'd lost forty years ago, on the strength of a
   ruling everyone believed was burned."
   / "Burned papers that turn up anyway. I'm starting to like these people."
3. "Haulers past the Class III gates carry sealed crates of blank ledger film
   one way, and never bring anything back."
   / "Blank film in, nothing out. Whatever they write, it stays there."
4. "Gas-giant skimmers talk about lights in the high clouds that never drift
   with the wind."
   / "Something floating in a gas giant on purpose. That's a lot of trouble
   for privacy."
5. "The film shipments all end at one giant past the edge of the charts. The
   Archive is in its clouds."
   / "There it is. Let's go and see who's been keeping score."

**Arrival** (speaker: THE ARCHIVE):
- *N.O.V.A., on approach:* "Lights in the clouds. Those are reading lamps,
  Captain. Hundreds of them."
- "Welcome to the Archive. We keep the record. All of it, including the parts
  people paid to have forgotten."
- "We don't sell copies and we don't take sides. We only ask why you need a
  page, and we write your answer down too."
- "You've come a long way with nothing to ask for. That's rare. Take our index
  of the old routes; it's the least-read thing we hold."
- *N.O.V.A.:* "Every deed, every verdict, every lie anyone tried to burn, all
  in one place. I could read here for a hundred years."
- *Farewell:* "The oldest page we hold mentions a place beyond the charts.
  We've never had a visitor from there. You could be the first."

**Bridges** (why the season's culprit wants this place):
- *rewrite a verdict:* "The Archive holds the full record of the trial that
  ruined their family, including what the court left out. One page from its
  stacks could overturn the verdict."
- *bury an old crime:* "Somewhere in the Archive's stacks is the one record
  that ties them to an old crime. While the Archive stands, the crime is never
  really buried."
- *prove them wrong:* "The Archive's copies show the registry, the courts and
  the guild all lied about the same thing for decades. Publish them, and the
  institution falls."
- *take the chair:* "The seat they want was promised to someone else, in a
  charter only the Archive still holds. Change that page, and the promise
  never existed."

## 4. The Neutral Ground

*Director note (never shown):* the decommissioned treaty station where old
enemies signed the peace that ended the last war. Each side built its own half,
in its own style; the halves meet at one central hall. It is still the only
place the factions meet unarmed. Its keepers, descended from both delegations,
enforce one rule: no weapon past the hall door.

**The rumour:** "Two factions at each other's throats both sent envoys out past
the gates, unarmed, on the same day."

**N.O.V.A., first time she hears it:** "Enemies, unarmed, on the same day,
going the same way. There's only one kind of place where that happens."

**Bearings** (clue / N.O.V.A.):
1. "Old war records mention a peace signed 'at the halfway hall', but no
   station by that name is on any chart."
   / "A peace signed somewhere nobody can find. Very careful, or very
   convenient."
2. "Envoys from rival factions refuel at the same deep depot, a day apart, and
   never speak."
   / "Two delegations, same road, same silence. They're going to the same
   table."
3. "A gunsmith grumbles that every envoy's ship comes back to her to have its
   weapons refitted."
   / "Everyone disarms to go there. Interesting kind of place."
4. "Traders describe a station built in two styles that don't match, joined in
   the middle by one hall."
   / "Two halves that don't match. Somebody built that from both ends and met
   in the middle."
5. "The envoys' fuel stops all point to one system past the edge of the
   charts. The Neutral Ground is there."
   / "Found it. Holster everything, Captain. I mean it."

**Arrival** (speaker: THE NEUTRAL GROUND):
- *N.O.V.A., on approach:* "Two stations joined at the hip. One half's all
  angles, the other's all curves. That's the treaty hall in the middle."
- "Weapons cold before the hall door. That's the only rule here, and it has
  held for sixty years."
- "My grandmother sat on one side of that table and my grandfather on the
  other. They signed the peace, then married. Somebody has to keep the hall."
- "The factions still send envoys here when talking is cheaper than fighting.
  Take our list of who's been talking to whom. Out there it's worth more than
  guns."
- *N.O.V.A.:* "Sixty years of enemies at one table, held together by one rule.
  That's braver than any war."
- *Farewell:* "Before you go: the treaty names a third delegation that never
  arrived. Their road ran past the charts. Maybe you'll find out why."

**Bridges:**
- *start a war:* "One shot fired inside the hall, and each side will blame the
  other. A war that starts at the Neutral Ground pays whoever sells to both."
- *take the chair:* "The hall's keeper speaks for the treaty, and the seat was
  promised to someone else's heir. Whoever holds it decides who gets to talk."
- *rewrite a verdict:* "The treaty tribunal ruled against their family when
  the war ended. The original minutes are still in the hall, and they say
  something else."
- *break a rival:* "Their rival is the envoy every faction trusts at the
  Neutral Ground. Disgrace them there, in front of everyone, and they're
  finished everywhere."
